# Canonical Contract Changes

Scope: Keep a changed semantic contract consistent across its wording, its inbound references, and any competing chronological records.

## Contract network

- A contract is a network of statements and inbound pointers, not one sentence; a local correction can leave sibling text or citing documents asserting the superseded model. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep; 2026-08-23)
- A sweep of old vocabulary distinguishes stale assertions from accurate negations, historical accounts, retirement notices, or changes covered elsewhere; raw match counts alone cannot make that distinction. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep; 2026-08-23)
- A document can contradict itself across a table, prose, or another section even when the changed paragraph is accurate. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep)
- When a procedure has been removed, an explicit retirement notice can preserve durable operational knowledge while preventing readers from treating the old procedure as current. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep)

## Append-only conflict evidence

- A zero-loss merge of conflicting log entries depends on entry identity, body comparison, identifier containment for superseded variants, and causal order; alphabetic order or raw counts alone cannot establish completeness. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep; 2026-08-23)
- Replacement claims are strongest when grounded in the underlying applied record and superseded models are explicitly negated where readers could mistake them for current. (src: canonical-contract-change-sweep; 2026-08-23)
